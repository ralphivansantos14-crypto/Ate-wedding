Their Story

How It Began

Their story began when her family became part of the church pastored by his parents. That's where their paths first crossed, and they became churchmates. At first, they were simply two young people who knew each other by name. But in 2012, their friendship slowly grew into something more meaningful, never knowing that God was already writing a beautiful story for them.

The Years Between

In 2015, friendship turned into love. They were young and still learning what love meant. Their journey was not always easy, there were seasons of holding on and seasons of letting go. Through it all, God was patiently shaping their hearts and teaching them that His timing is always greater.

The Chapter That Stayed

On March 23, 2021, after finding their way back to each other, they chose to begin again, this time with more understanding, patience, and faith. They realized that their love was not only about choosing each other, but about choosing God together.

The Proposal

On their fifth anniversary in March 2026, he took her to La Union, one of their favorite places to watch the sunset. As the sun went down, he asked the question that would change their story forever. She said yes. And on December 4, 2026, the two people who once simply knew each other by name are preparing to stand before God and promise each other a lifetime.
